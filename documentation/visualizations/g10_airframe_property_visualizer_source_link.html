Data source
Dielectric Manufacturing, “Material Properties of Thermoset Glass Epoxy” G10 table. Values in this viewer are tied to the published G10 parameters in that table. The 3D mapping assumes tube lengthwise = axial and crosswise = circumferential.
How to read the terms on a tube
LengthwiseAlong the rocket body axis. This is usually the direction that sees axial tension/compression and global bending stresses.
CrosswiseAround the circumference / hoop direction. This is related to split resistance and hoop loading.
FlatwiseNormal to laminate faces. On a tube wall, this is shown as radial through-wall compression.
EdgewiseCompression in the laminate plane, loaded through an exposed edge. On a tube wall, this is shown tangentially along the wall edge.

Important caveat: the linked data is for G10 glass-epoxy laminate stock. A real rocket airframe tube may be rolled, convolute wound, filament wound, or built with a specific cloth orientation. Use this viewer as a direction/terminology aid, not as a substitute for the exact tube manufacturer’s datasheet.
